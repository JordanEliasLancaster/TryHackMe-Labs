## 🧠 Summary
This introductory SOC-level room covers the role and responsibilities of a **Junior (Tier 1) Security Analyst**.  
It explains how analysts monitor network traffic, detect suspicious activity, and escalate incidents to Tier 2 and Tier 3 teams.

---

## 🧩 Key Takeaways
- **SOC Structure:** Tier 1 (Monitoring), Tier 2 (Incident Response), Tier 3 (Threat Hunting)  
- **Main Duties:** Monitor traffic logs, investigate alerts, escalate confirmed incidents  
- **Tools Covered:** IDS (Intrusion Detection Systems), SIEM overview  
- **Career Insight:** Typical Tier 1 responsibilities, required certifications (Security+), and team collaboration
